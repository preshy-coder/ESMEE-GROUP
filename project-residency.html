The Residency

By Esmee Group Innovations

Overview

A private residence built around light and line

The Residency is a private residential project delivered end-to-end by Esmee Group — from architectural concept through construction supervision to final finishes. Clean geometric massing, full-height glazing and warm exterior stonework combine to create a home that feels both modern and grounded.

The design concept centres on connecting interior living spaces to the outdoors, using large glass frontages, layered balconies and a considered material palette of dark cladding, natural stone and white render.

Design Concept

Massing, materials, and light

The layout stacks living spaces to maximise natural light and views, while deep overhangs and stone-clad columns anchor the structure visually. Balcony terraces on the upper level extend the living space outward, blurring the line between inside and out.

Landscaping was designed alongside the architecture rather than after it — paved pathways, low hedges and lawn areas were planned to complement the home's geometry from the earliest concept sketches.
